The business side (what each status means, who may cancel or refund, the animated flows from
checkout to delivery) is on [docs.merkaly.io/pedidos](https://docs.merkaly.io/pedidos), with the lifecycles
[Estado de la venta](https://docs.merkaly.io/ciclos/sale-status), [Estado de la compra](https://docs.merkaly.io/ciclos/purchase-status) and
[Estado del envío](https://docs.merkaly.io/ciclos/shipment-status). This file keeps the code side: services, fields and triggers.

A sale is followed through three independent domains. What the buyer agreed at checkout
(recipient, address, shipping fee, import taxes, delivery estimate) is `sale.delivery`; the parcels actually
sent are `logistics.shipments`. Each one owns its state; the sale only
summarizes them.

| Domain | Entity | Answers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| finance | `finance.payments` | Did the buyer pay? | `PENDING` `PAID` `DISPUTED` `CANCELLED` `REFUND_PENDING` `REFUND_PROCESSING` `REFUNDED` `CHARGED_BACK` |
| finance | `finance.chargebacks` | Was a payment charged back? | `OPEN` `IN_DISPUTE` `WON` `LOST` |
| order | `order.purchases` | Was it bought from the supplier? | `TO_PLACE` `PLACED` `AWAITING_PAYMENT` `PAID` `SHIPPED` `FAILED` `CANCELLED` |
| logistics | `logistics.shipments` | Where is the parcel? | `PENDING` `IN_TRANSIT` `DELIVERED` `EXCEPTION` |
| order | `order.sales` | Summary for merchant and buyer | derived, see below |

Future work grows inside each domain: returns (a `RETURN`
shipment), carriers, labels and tracking webhooks in logistics.

## Sale status (stored, derived)

`order.sales.status` is never set by hand. `OrderSaleStatusService.recompute(sale)` derives it
and stores it (the status plugin keeps the history) after every payment, purchase or shipment
change. Lists and filters read the stored field.

| Status | Rule (first match wins) |
|---|---|
| `CANCELLED` | the sale was cancelled (`cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy` `MERCHANT`, `BUYER` or `SUPPLIER`), the payment is `CANCELLED`, or it is `REFUNDED` / `CHARGED_BACK` with no live purchase paid at the supplier (`PAID` / `SHIPPED`) |
| `AWAITING_PAYMENT` | payment not `PAID` / `DISPUTED` (nor `REFUNDED` / `CHARGED_BACK`) |
| `DELIVERED` (merchant) | no live purchase and every outbound shipment `DELIVERED` (merchant shipments only) |
| `SUPPLIER_CANCELLED` | no live purchase and a purchase placed at the supplier was cancelled: the merchant cancels and refunds, or orders again (attention `SUPPLIER_CANCELLED`) |
| `TO_ORDER` | paid, no live purchase placed yet (none, or all `TO_PLACE` / `FAILED`). Whether it is paid is the payment (`finance.payments`), never this status |
| `DELIVERED` | every live purchase shipped and every outbound shipment `DELIVERED` |
| `SHIPPED` | every live purchase shipped (`SHIPPED`) and every shipment `IN_TRANSIT` / `DELIVERED` |
| `TO_PAY` | a live purchase is placed at the supplier but unpaid there (`AWAITING_PAYMENT`): the merchant pays it in the supplier account |
| `ORDERED` | otherwise (placed and paid at the supplier, waiting for it to ship) |

Live purchases are the ones not deleted and not `CANCELLED`. Shipments still `PENDING` (a
tracking number without movement, e.g. a warehouse alias of the real parcel) are ignored, as
long as another shipment has movement. A shipment in `EXCEPTION` never moves the sale on its
own: the sale stays where it is and the admin attention shows it, so the merchant decides
(refund or order again).

**Supplier cancellation** (AliExpress push or sync reads an order cancelled or closed unpaid). One
purchase is one request at AliExpress, answered with one order per item (`supplierOrder.orders[]`).
When some of its orders are cancelled the purchase stays live and `OrderSalePurchaseRefundFlow`
refunds their items after the commit: price plus their share of the checkout freight and taxes, one
refund per cancelled order (also run at the end of the sale supplier sync, which asks again the ones
still owed). When every order is cancelled the purchase becomes `CANCELLED` and, when the sale has no
other live purchase, `OrderSupplierOrderService` cancels the sale through the same path as the
merchant cancel (`OrderSaleCancelFlow`, `by: SUPPLIER`). It is idempotent (a cancelled sale is skipped)
and runs in the tenant of the push. The sale refund is what is left (paid minus the item refunds
the provider took; the ones still owed are replaced by it, never asked twice). Two syncs that cancel the last purchases
close together cancel the sale once (the second finds it cancelled). A failed
automatic cancellation is logged and written on `supplierOrder.error`, and the sale shows
`SUPPLIER_CANCELLED` (never back to `TO_ORDER`): the merchant cancels and refunds, or orders again. Business side: [El proveedor cancela](https://docs.merkaly.io/flujos/proveedor-cancela).

After an online payment the sale is procured and ordered at the supplier automatically
(unpaid, never forced) in two steps through the queue `merkaly.api-procurement`: `order.sale.paid`
procures, then the command `order.purchase.place` places each purchase on its own. AliExpress
throttling (`ApiCallLimit` included) and other transient failures are retried by the broker (30 s, 5
times, then `.dlq`; the delay is fixed per queue in `@merkaly/nest`). Account and buyer errors
(`A006_INVALID_ACCOUNT_INFO`, recipient CPF, address) are never retried. If the procurement stops the
reason is only logged: a checkout sale `TO_ORDER` for more than 30 minutes with AliExpress items and
no purchase ever shows the admin attention `AUTO_PROCUREMENT_FAILED`; a placement that stops leaves
its purchase `FAILED` (`SUPPLIER_ORDER_FAILED`). The manual procurement and placement are the fallback. Business side: [Compra automática al proveedor](https://docs.merkaly.io/flujos/compra-automatica).

Merchant actions:

- **Cancel**: sets `cancelledAt` and `cancelledBy` (shown on the admin history `SALE_CANCELLED` entry); a pending payment is cancelled, a paid one owes its refund
  (`REFUND_PENDING`), asked right after the commit (`REFUND_PROCESSING` until the provider confirms it).
  409 while a live purchase is paid at the supplier (`PAID` / `SHIPPED`). The purchases not paid
  yet (`TO_PLACE`, `PLACED`, `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, `FAILED`) are cancelled in the same transaction
  (`OrderSaleCancelFlow` calls `OrderSupplierOrderService.cancelForSale`). AliExpress DS has no cancel
  call, so its order is left unpaid (it closes by itself) and the purchase `supplierOrder.error`
  says not to pay it. A sync or push of a `CANCELLED` purchase never revives it: it keeps the raw
  status and, when the supplier reports it paid or shipped, sets `supplierOrder.paidAfterCancelAt`
  (attention `SUPPLIER_PAID_AFTER_CANCEL`, any sale status). The flag clears when the supplier
  reports the order cancelled again (refunded).
  Before a pending one is cancelled its payable charges are removed (through payments.merkaly.io)
  (`OrderSaleCancelFlow`, before its transaction), so a Pix cannot be paid afterwards; 409 if it was just paid.
- There is no manual "mark as delivered": the delivery comes from the supplier tracking or the
  buyer confirmation (the money of a sale is released for withdrawal `WITHDRAWAL_HOLD_DAYS` after
  its delivery, see `src/domain/payment/README.md`).

Buyer actions:

- **Create** (`POST /store/account/orders`): 503 before any write while the organization cannot
  sell (`PaymentAccountService.requireSelling()` through `SellingGuard`, see `src/domain/payment/README.md`).
- **Cancel** (`POST /store/account/orders/:id/cancellation`, own `CHECKOUT` order): same path and
  checks as the merchant cancel, no approval. Allowed in `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, or with the payment
  `PAID` in `SALE_CANCELLABLE_STATUSES` while no live purchase is paid at the supplier; the refund is
  the whole payment.
- **Refund** (`POST /store/account/orders/:id/refund`, own `CHECKOUT` order): `DELIVERED`, payment
  `PAID` and less than `SALE_REFUND_DAYS` since the last shipment `deliveredAt` (closed without a
  delivery date). `OrderSaleCancelFlow.refundDeliveredSale` cancels the sale with `cancelledBy`
  `BUYER`, keeps its purchases (already paid at the supplier, still a cost in the summary:
  `isSaleClosed` only closes a sale without live purchases) and refunds the whole payment through
  `OrderSaleRefundFlow`.
- Both check `utils/cancellation.ts` (`getBuyerCancellation`) on the server, 409 otherwise, and are
  idempotent: on a sale the buyer already cancelled they answer the order again, and the transaction
  re-checks it, so a double click never refunds twice. The buyer `fulfillment.cancellation`
  (`{ action: CANCEL | REFUND | null, until }`, `until` only for `REFUND`) is the same rule, so the
  store never recomputes it.
- **Confirm received** (`POST /store/account/orders/:id/receipt`, own order), like AliExpress
  "confirm received": in `SHIPPED`, or in `DELIVERED` while no shipment has `receivedBy` `BUYER` and
  `WITHDRAWAL_HOLD_DAYS` have not passed since the delivery date (409 otherwise).

The buyer confirmation adds a `DELIVERED` event to every open outbound shipment (or creates a shipment
when there is none, e.g. products without supplier) and sets `receivedBy` `BUYER`;
the sale becomes `DELIVERED` (`OrderSaleReceiptFlow`, one transaction). After the commit the flow
tells each supplier that needs it (`OrderSupplierOrderService.notifyDelivered`): AliExpress has no such call (answers "not
supported"; the order closes at AliExpress when confirmed in the account or when its protection
window ends). A failed notification never undoes the confirmation; it stays on `supplierOrder.error`.

Sales are only created by the store checkout (`source: CHECKOUT`) or the import (`IMPORT`); the
admin cannot create or edit sales, items, purchases or customers by hand.

Buyer stage groups (`GET /store/account/orders?stage=`) map onto the stored status:
`AWAITING_PAYMENT`, `RECEIVED` = `TO_ORDER` + `TO_PAY` + `SUPPLIER_CANCELLED` (the buyer never sees the supplier), `IN_PREPARATION` = `ORDERED`, `SHIPPED`, `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`.
The buyer `fulfillment` carries `orderedAt` (last live supplier order placed, null before): the
store shows delivery dates counted from it and only an approximate window in days before.
It also carries `packages` (`OrderBuyerPackage`, built by `utils/buyer.package.ts`): the live items
of a purchase grouped by the shipments that carry them (items not shipped yet together), then one
package per cancelled supplier order (cancelled last). Each has the sale item ids, its `stage`
(`OrderBuyerStageEnum`, from the sale status, the least advanced status of its supplier orders and
its shipments), its neutral `shipments`, `deliveryDays` (slowest `delivery.lines` quote of its items)
and `refund` (`{ amount, status }` of the `PURCHASE` refund of its cancelled order, else `null`). A
purchase whose orders are not matched to items yet is one package. Items without a purchase share
one last package with the merchant shipments. Refunds are read once per batch.
On the store the sale `pricing` is `OrderBuyerPricing` (`OrderSalePaymentService.getBuyerPricingBySales`):
`total` stays items + shipping + `taxes` (what the store receives, as on the admin) and it adds `fee` (the
service fee: `payment.fee` once charged, the current Pix fee while `AWAITING_PAYMENT` without a
charge, 0 when confirmed by hand; `null` while the fees are not loaded) and `buyerTotal`
(`total` + `fee`, what the buyer pays). The payments of a page are read in one query.

Each sale also stores its `source`: `CHECKOUT` (store), `ADMIN` (reserved: manual sales are disabled) or
`IMPORT` (order placed outside Merkaly; its supplier order ids live on the purchase). Imported
sales are left out of store metrics and payment flows.

## Purchase status (the supplier order)

The purchase is the order at the supplier. Its status replaces `purchase.supplierOrder.status`;
`supplierOrder` keeps only the provider data (order ids, reference, raw status, error, dates).
`supplierOrder.error` is `{ code, message }` or `null`, always written as a whole: `message` is the
English detail (log) and `code` the stable `SupplierErrorCodeEnum` code when the provider error has
one (e.g. `SUPPLIER_RECIPIENT_DOCUMENT_INVALID`), so frontends translate it; `code` is `null` for
unclassified errors and notes (placement warning, unmapped status, cancel and delivery notes).

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `TO_PLACE` | created by procurement, not placed yet |
| `PLACED` | created at the supplier, payment state unknown |
| `AWAITING_PAYMENT` | created unpaid: the merchant pays it in the supplier account |
| `PAID` | paid at the supplier, not shipped yet |
| `SHIPPED` | the supplier handed it to logistics (from here the shipments tell the story) |
| `FAILED` | the supplier rejected it; it can be placed again |
| `CANCELLED` | cancelled with the sale (not paid at the supplier yet) or by the supplier (every order of the request) |

AliExpress mapping (`aliexpress.trade.ds.order.get`): `PLACE_ORDER_SUCCESS` → `AWAITING_PAYMENT`;
`WAIT_SELLER_EXAMINE_MONEY`, `RISK_CONTROL`, `WAIT_SELLER_SEND_GOODS` → `PAID`;
`SELLER_PART_SEND_GOODS`, `WAIT_BUYER_ACCEPT_GOODS` → `SHIPPED`; `FINISH` with
`BUYER_ACCEPT_GOODS` → `SHIPPED` and its shipments `DELIVERED`; `FINISH` closed without shipping
or `IN_CANCEL` → `CANCELLED`. One request comes back as one order per item: each keeps its own
status, items and charges in `supplierOrder.orders[]`, and the purchase takes the least advanced
live one (`CANCELLED` when every order is).

## Shipments (`logistics.shipments`)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sale` | the sale |
| `purchase` | the purchase that produced it (null for merchant shipments) |
| `items` | `{ item, quantity }`: sale items inside, from the supplier orders sharing its tracking number (empty: unknown) |
| `source` | `SUPPLIER` (read from the supplier) or `MERCHANT`/`BUYER` (confirmed by hand) |
| `status` | `PENDING`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `DELIVERED`, `EXCEPTION` |
| `tracking` | `{ number, carrier, url }`, raw values (admin only); null when confirmed without tracking |
| `shippedAt`, `deliveredAt` | carrier dates of the first transit and the delivery (the status `history` keeps when Merkaly saw it) |
| `receivedBy` | who reported the delivery: `SUPPLIER` (provider says received), `CARRIER` (tracking), `MERCHANT`, `BUYER` |
| `events[]` | tracking timeline, newest first |

One request may ship in several parcels and several orders may share one parcel (AliExpress
consolidates Choice items): one shipment per tracking number, with the items of every order it
carries.

Each tracking event:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| `date` | event date and time |
| `description` | provider text, as received (admin only) |
| `location` | `Curitiba, PR`, when given |
| `code` | normalized: `POSTED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `CUSTOMS`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, `DELIVERED`, `EXCEPTION` |

Sync and import add only new events (deduplicated by date and description), never remove old
ones. The shipment status follows its newest event; when the provider reports the order as
received without a delivery event, the shipment becomes `DELIVERED` anyway.

### What the buyer sees

The store never shows the supplier (rule: no AliExpress, Cainiao or supplier names). It reads a
neutral view of the shipments: status, dates, the tracking number, real carriers only
(supplier-branded ones become `Transportadora`), tracking pages outside supplier domains, and
events as `{ at, code, location }` without the provider description. The store renders each
`code` with its own text.

## Triggers

Each flow (`src/domain/order/flows/`) recomputes the sale status explicitly inside its transaction;
no purchase or shipment event triggers it.

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| checkout or import creates a sale | payment `PENDING` → sale `AWAITING_PAYMENT` |
| payment confirmed (online charge through payments.merkaly.io or AliExpress import, never by hand), cancelled or refunded | recompute |
| online payment confirmed (charge read through payments.merkaly.io, committed) | `OrderSalePaymentSyncFlow` publishes `order.sale.paid`; the queue `merkaly.api-procurement` runs the automatic procurement (`OrderSaleAutoProcureFlow`), which sends `order.purchase.place` per purchase after the commit, and each supplier order (`OrderPurchaseAutoPlaceFlow`); transient failures retried by the broker, then `.dlq`; a procurement stop is only logged (attention `AUTO_PROCUREMENT_FAILED` after the grace), a placement stop stays on the purchase |
| procurement creates purchases (`TO_PLACE`) | recompute |
| supplier order placed, synced (manual or AliExpress push) or imported | purchase status, shipments and events; recompute |
| merchant or buyer cancels, buyer refunds a delivered sale, buyer confirms received | recompute |
| merchant or buyer cancels | purchases not paid at the supplier → `CANCELLED` (a buyer refund after the delivery keeps them) |
| supplier cancels every order of the only live purchase | sale cancelled and refunded (merchant cancel path): what is left |
| supplier cancels some orders of a purchase (or a purchase, others live) | their items refunded (products, freight and taxes), payment stays `PAID` |
