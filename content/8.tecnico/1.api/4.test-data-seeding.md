The legacy `seed full` and `seed minimal` commands stay retired (ENG-21): they dropped the
tenant database, recreated organization settings, read Auth0 users and generated random catalog.
The only seeding that exists is the **orders** fixture, approved by the Founder so the admin
(orders list, sales cockpit) and the store account area have data on development tenants.

## Commands

```bash
pnpm console seed orders <org> [--count 40] [--user <auth0 user id>]
pnpm console seed orders:clear <org>
```

- `seed orders <org>` creates `--count` sales (default 40, from 12 to 400) with their payments and
  supplier purchases. When the tenant already has a seeded batch it is cleared first, so a rerun
  replaces the batch instead of adding to it.
- `--user` is the buyer user id stored on the sales (`sale.user`). The default is the first user
  of the tenant (`account.users`, by id), so the store account area of that user shows the orders.
  The user is only referenced, never created or changed.
- `seed orders:clear <org>` deletes the seeded batch and nothing else (see Tagging).
- Options use the `--count 40` form (not `--count=40`).

## Safety guard

Checked by the command right before any write:

1. `NODE_ENV` must not be `production`.
2. The tenant is selected through the CLS `identity`, exactly as requests do; a tenant without a
   database (no collections) is refused. `seed orders` also refuses a tenant without active
   products with priced variants.
3. Before writing, the command prints the cluster label, the database name and the document count
   of every collection; after writing it prints the counts again.

## What it writes (and never writes)

Writes, in the selected tenant only: `order.sales`, `order.purchases`, `finance.payments` and
`contact.customers`. Creating sales and purchases advances the order and payment sequence counters
(`settings`, document `sequence`), as any real order does; clearing
does not rewind them.

Never: drops a database or collection, creates catalog (it sells the tenant's existing active
products), touches Auth0, users, memberships, credentials, organization settings or branding,
assets, or calls a provider (AliExpress, payments.merkaly.io).

Records are created through the order services and flows (`OrderSaleService.create`, the pending
payment, `OrderSalePaymentConfirmFlow`, `update`, `OrderPurchaseService.create`; the cancel is the
merchant one without its provider lookups, then `OrderSaleRefundFlow`), so totals, payments and status
history are the real ones. The supplier order placed at AliExpress is written with
`OrderPurchaseRepository.setSupplierOrder`, the same write the supplier order service does after
calling the provider, but with fake AliExpress-shaped ids. Afterwards the creation and history
dates are moved back to the planned timeline (the services stamp "now").

Data: Faker pt-BR with a fixed seed (same catalog and count, same orders; dates are relative to
the run), recipients with valid-format CPFs, AliExpress city names, BR phones and CEPs, all money
in BRL cents. Item prices are the catalog `retailPrice`; purchase costs are the catalog
`supplierPrice`; shipping lines are quoted freights.

## Tagging and clear

Every seeded customer and sale recipient uses the reserved domain `@seed.merkaly.test` (RFC 2606
`.test`: no real buyer can own it). `seed orders:clear` selects only:

1. sales whose `shipping.recipient.email` ends with `@seed.merkaly.test`;
2. purchases whose `sale` is one of those sales;
3. payments whose `order` is one of those sales or purchases;
4. customers with an `@seed.merkaly.test` email that no other sale references (a referenced one
   is kept and reported as a warning).

It deletes them in one transaction and prints what it deleted.

## Scenarios

A batch of 40 is spread over the last 30 days of `createdAt` (other counts keep the proportions,
at least one each):

| Scenario | Sales | Sale status | Cockpit exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Payment pending, created in the last 7 days | 5 | AWAITING_PAYMENT | none (awaiting the gateway) |
| Payment pending, older than 7 days | 3 | AWAITING_PAYMENT | none (abandoned) |
| Paid, no purchase | 5 | PAID | TO_ORDER |
| Paid, purchase TO_PLACE | 2 | PAID | TO_ORDER |
| Purchase AWAITING_PAYMENT | 4 | ORDERED | SUPPLIER_PAYMENT_PENDING |
| Purchase FAILED | 3 | PAID | SUPPLIER_ORDER_FAILED |
| Purchase PAID, placed in the last 7 days | 4 | ORDERED | none |
| Purchase PAID, placed more than 7 days ago | 3 | ORDERED | SHIPMENT_DELAYED |
| Purchase SHIPPED, shipment IN_TRANSIT with a tracking timeline (Correios or Cainiao) | 4 | SHIPPED | none |
| Purchase SHIPPED, received: shipments DELIVERED | 4 | DELIVERED | none |
| Cancelled before payment (payment CANCELLED) | 2 | CANCELLED | none |
| Cancelled after payment (payment REFUNDED, by hand) | 1 | CANCELLED | none |

Sale statuses are derived ([order lifecycle](/tecnico/api/architecture/order-lifecycle)): the seeder sets payments,
purchase statuses and shipments through the real services and lets the sale recompute.
